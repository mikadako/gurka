you will make a web cardgame 'gurka'.

standard deck, no jokers.

2 - 8 players. ask for number of players before game starts.

computer handles all players except me. invent funny names for all the computer-handled players.

you do not need figure out a strategy for the computer-handled player,
they can play randomly (but within the rules of course)

each player get 6 cards.

a round (a trick) is this:

  first player plays any card. this is the 'high' card. colours do not
  matter at all in this game, only the rank. Ace is a high card and has rank 14.

  next player in turn has to play: either his lowest card OR his card
  that is his card that the next higher card than the previous 'high'
  card. if he chooses to play a higher card, this is now the 'high'
  card to which the following player has to apply the same procedure.

  when each player has played one card each, he who played the highest
  card starts the next round.

  note: the only time you can play the same card as the 'high' card,
  is when that is your lowest card. for the purpose of deciding who
  starts the next round, playing your lowest card same as the current
  'high' card does not give your card the status of 'high'.

  the player that ends up with the highest card in the last trick has
  lost that round and get as many points as the rank of his card (it is bad to get points).

  if several players ends up with the same highest card they all get as many points.

  any player who ends up with an ace in the last trick has lost the game and leaves the table.

  any player that gets 50 points or more is out of the game and has to leave the table.

  after each hand has been played and some points has been assigned or
  some out-due-to-ace situation has been handled, it is the next
  players turn in clockwise direction to start the next hand.
